Apple Health

Apple Health access in Vowzy is optional. It is used only for habits that you choose to verify with step proof.

What Vowzy Reads

Vowzy can ask permission to read your Apple Health step count. This lets selected step-proof habits confirm whether today's step goal has been reached before saving a completion.

Vowzy does not write workouts, health samples, or medical records to Apple Health.

Optional

Step-proof habits only

Health data is used only when a habit is set to Step Proof.

Private by design

No health writing

Vowzy reads step count for verification and does not write health samples.

Review Apple Health Access

iOS may not show a separate Apple Health option inside the Vowzy app settings page. You can review step access from Apple Health's privacy controls instead.

  1. Open the Health app.
  2. Choose Sharing.
  3. Choose Apps.
  4. Choose Vowzy.
  5. Turn Steps access on or off.

You can also check Settings, Privacy & Security, Health, then Vowzy.

Using Vowzy Without Apple Health

If Apple Health is unavailable or access is off, Vowzy still supports quick-tap habits, proof check-ins, guided journeys, custom journeys, reminders, iCloud sync, and daily health summaries.

If a step-proof habit cannot read Apple Health steps, you can switch that specific habit back to quick-tap completion from the proof screen.

Wellbeing Note
